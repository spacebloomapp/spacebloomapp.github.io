What SpaceBloom does

Junk Cleaner

With Usage access, see the real cache size of each app and open its settings to clear it (Android only allows clearing another app's cache there). Find APK files, large media files and empty folders, review them, and delete only what you choose.

Battery Information

Level, charging status, power source, health, temperature, voltage and current, as reported by your phone. Values your device doesn't report are shown as not available.

Speaker Clean

Plays a low-frequency tone for 30 seconds, with optional vibration, that may help push out water or dust. Results vary.

Network Test

Shows your connection type and measures real download speed and latency with a short test download.

App Usage

With Usage access, see your screen time per app for today and the last 7 days.

SpaceBloom is free and supported by ads. Usage access, media access and file information stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.
