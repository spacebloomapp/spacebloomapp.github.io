Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the SpaceBloom Android app (package com.superclean.mls.spacebloom) handles information. SpaceBloom has no user accounts and no servers of its own. The app's tools work on your device, and SpaceBloom does not collect, store or upload your personal data. The only third-party services the app uses are Google AdMob (ads) and Cloudflare (the network speed test), described below.

Usage access (App Usage and app cache sizes)

If you turn on Android's "Usage access" for SpaceBloom, the app reads usage statistics and storage statistics on your device only. It uses them to show your screen time per app and each app's cache size. This information is never uploaded or shared. You can turn Usage access off at any time in Android Settings.

Photos, videos, audio and files

If you allow access to photos, videos and audio, or pick files or a folder with Android's file picker, SpaceBloom uses that access only to find files you may choose to delete: APK files, large media files and empty folders. Files are never uploaded or shared. Nothing is deleted unless you select it, and media deletions go through Android's own confirmation dialog.

Battery information

Battery details (level, status, health, temperature, voltage, current) are read from Android on your device only and are not uploaded.

Speaker Clean

Speaker Clean plays a generated sound and, if you choose, a vibration pattern. It does not record anything and does not use the microphone.

Network Test

The speed test downloads test data from Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com) to measure download speed and latency. As with any web request, Cloudflare receives your IP address. SpaceBloom sends no personal data with the test. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What SpaceBloom does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No access to your contacts.
  • No location permission.
  • No notifications.
  • We do not sell your data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SpaceBloom shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), your IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, app interaction data and diagnostic information. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, SpaceBloom asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options.

Opting out of personalised ads

On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads. There you can delete your advertising ID (or, on older versions, opt out of ads personalisation). Your choice applies to ads in SpaceBloom and other apps.

Security

Data sent over the network by the app, including the speed test and ad requests, is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Children

SpaceBloom is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
